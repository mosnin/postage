# App Idea: PostSyncer Clone

## Product Concept
A social media scheduling and management SaaS platform that allows creators, teams, and agencies to compose, schedule, and publish content across 11+ social media platforms from a single dashboard. Includes AI-powered content generation, unified comment inbox, analytics, and team collaboration tools.

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Content creators, influencers, solopreneurs (1-3 person teams)
- **Secondary**: Social media managers at small-to-mid businesses
- **Tertiary**: Agencies managing multiple client brands/workspaces

## Core Problem
Managing social media presence across multiple platforms is time-consuming, fragmented, and difficult to coordinate in teams. Users switch between 5-11 different apps, lose track of scheduled posts, and can't easily collaborate on content approval.

## Solution
One platform to schedule posts across all major social networks, with AI assistance for content creation, a unified inbox for managing replies, and team approval workflows.

## Supported Platforms
Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Telegram, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon

## V1 Scope
- Multi-platform post scheduling and publishing
- Visual content calendar with drag-and-drop
- AI caption generator and content agent
- Media library (upload, manage, reuse assets)
- Unified comments inbox
- Basic analytics per platform
- Team workspaces with role-based permissions
- Approval workflows
- Billing with 3 paid tiers (Starter, Pro, Pro Plus)
- Marketing website with pricing page
- REST API for external integrations
- MCP integration for AI assistants

## Out of Scope for V1
- Mobile native apps (web only, responsive)
- White-label reselling
- AI video generation (UI placeholder only)
- Deep CRM/audience management beyond basic contact tracking
- Viral video library (placeholder)
- Blog-to-social automation
- Auto-plug / X Communities (UI placeholders)
